// how we treat each other

Four principles that make the Clarity Community a place people actually want to come back to.

// 01

Beginner questions are the best questions

There are no stupid questions in the Clarity Community. Every expert here was once a beginner with a folder called "stuff". We celebrate curious newcomers and answer every question with patience and respect.

// 02

Share what actually works, not what sounds impressive

We prefer honest "here's what worked for me" over polished productivity flex. Real systems, real struggles, real progress — that's what helps people. Leave the performative productivity at the door.

// 03

Lift people up, never put them down

Digital disorganization is often tied to stress, grief, ADHD, or just a really busy life. We meet people where they are with empathy, encouragement, and zero judgment. This is a safe space to be honest about your mess.

// 04

Celebrate every win, no matter how small

Deleted one duplicate file? That counts. Archived 10 emails? That counts. Created your first proper folder? Absolutely counts. Progress is progress, and we celebrate all of it — loudly and sincerely.

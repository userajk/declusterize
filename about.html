About deClusterize

Built for the quietly
overwhelmed.

We started deClusterize because the problem wasn't a lack of tools — it was too many of them. We write calm, focused, step-by-step guides that cut through the noise so you can get back to living.

The problem we set out to solve

Most of us juggle several devices, a couple of cloud accounts and an inbox that never quite empties. Every new tool that promises to help adds another thing to maintain. Complexity compounds. Clarity vanishes.

deClusterize was founded on a single observation: people don't need another app — they need a guide. Plain-English instructions that meet them where they are, say exactly what to tap or click, and move them forward in 15-minute sessions.

No dashboards to dread. No 40-step setups. Just progress, every day.

What we stand for

Our core values

Respect for your time

Every guide must earn its place by saving you more time than it takes to read. If it doesn't, it doesn't get published.

Privacy by default

There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to install. The guides run in your browser, checklist progress stays on your device, and we never see your files, photos or inbox.

Radical simplicity

Complexity is the enemy of action. We obsessively strip away everything that isn't essential until what's left is obvious.

Progress over perfection

An imperfect system you actually use beats a perfect one you don't. We meet you where you are and move forward from there.

Who's behind it

deClusterize is a small, independent project — not a company with a product team. Every guide is written by hand, walked through on a real device, and links to the original Apple, Google or Microsoft documentation it draws on. Spotted something that's out of date? Tell us and it gets fixed.

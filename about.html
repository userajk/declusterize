About deClusterize

Built for the quietly
overwhelmed.

We started deClusterize because the problem wasn't a lack of tools — it was too many of them. We build calm, focused systems that cut through the noise so you can get back to living.

The problem we set out to solve

The average person manages 3.2 devices, 2 cloud storage accounts, and 4,700 unread emails. Every new tool that promises to help adds another thing to maintain. Complexity compounds. Clarity vanishes.

deClusterize was founded on a single observation: people don't need another app — they need a guide. A system that meets them where they are, identifies what actually matters, and moves them forward in 15-minute sessions.

No dashboards to dread. No 40-step setups. Just progress, every day.

What we stand for

Our core values

Respect for your time

Every feature we ship must earn its place by saving you more time than it takes to learn. If it doesn't, it doesn't ship.

Privacy by default

Your data is yours. We don't sell it, we don't train on it without consent, and we store only what's needed to deliver the plan.

Radical simplicity

Complexity is the enemy of action. We obsessively strip away everything that isn't essential until what's left is obvious.

Progress over perfection

An imperfect system you actually use beats a perfect one you don't. We meet you where you are and move forward from there.

Once you've completed your core plan — typically within 30 days — deClusterize automatically transitions you to Maintenance Mode. This is a lightweight weekly routine of two or three micro-tasks that takes under 5 minutes and keeps everything you built from sliding back into chaos.

Every quarter, your plan refreshes with a short re-assessment so it stays aligned with however your life has changed.

// the science behind 15 minutes

Short sessions aren't a compromise — they're the strategy. Here's why the 15-minute constraint produces better long-term results than marathon organising sessions.

66 days

How long habits actually take to form

A landmark University College London study found the average time to form a new habit is 66 days — not the often-cited 21. But the study also found that missing an occasional session had no measurable impact on habit formation. What mattered was consistent repetition over time, not perfection.

A 15-minute daily session is easy enough to keep up even on busy days. A two-hour session is easy to skip. That's why our method prioritises frequency over intensity — you're not trying to organise everything at once, you're building the habit of organising regularly.

// Source: Lally et al. (2010), European Journal of Social Psychology

40%

Decision fatigue kills organising sessions

Research by Baumeister and colleagues found that decision-making depletes a finite cognitive resource — and that the quality of decisions deteriorates significantly after a long session of choices. Organising is full of micro-decisions: keep or delete? Move or rename? Archive or action?

By limiting sessions to 15 minutes and scoping each task so tightly that there are almost no decisions to make, deClusterize avoids the decision fatigue that causes most self-directed organising attempts to stall halfway through. You finish each session with energy left over — which means you come back tomorrow.

// Source: Baumeister et al. (1998), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

Archive the existing backlog
Set up a two-folder action system

Photos & Media

Most people have thousands of photos scattered across multiple devices, apps, and backup drives. This area consolidates, cleans, and creates an album structure you'll actually use.

Slow devices full of apps you don't use and files you don't need are a daily drag on your productivity and mood. This area frees storage and keeps your devices running cleanly.

Audit and uninstall unused apps
Clear app caches and temp files
Offload photos and videos to cloud
Review and remove large files
Set up automatic device backup

Cloud storage is only useful when it's organised. This area consolidates your cloud accounts, removes redundant files, and makes sure the things that matter are actually backed up.

Audit all connected cloud accounts
Remove duplicate cloud copies
Organise shared Drive or Dropbox folders
Set up a backup-of-backups system
Revoke access for apps no longer used

// privacy & what we access

We know you're trusting us with your digital life. Here's a clear, honest account of what we access, what we don't, and why.

🔍

Metadata only, by default

By default, deClusterize reads only file and email metadata — names, sizes, dates, and folder structure. We never read the contents of your documents, photos, or messages unless you explicitly enable a feature that requires it. You can review all active permissions at any time in Account Settings.

🔒

Your data stays yours

We do not sell, share, or train AI models on your personal data without your explicit consent. All integrations use secure OAuth — we never ask for your passwords. You can revoke any integration instantly from your settings, and your account data is deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.

🛡️

Enterprise-grade security

All data in transit is protected by TLS 1.3. Sensitive fields are encrypted at rest with AES-256. We conduct regular third-party penetration tests and maintain a responsible disclosure programme. If you ever have a security concern, contact us directly at info@declusterize.com.

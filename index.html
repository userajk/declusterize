Common Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is deClusterize really free to use?

Yes — all guides, checklists, and the full 5-step method are completely free. No sign-up required, no strings attached.

Do I need to download anything?

No download needed. deClusterize works entirely in your browser — on your phone, tablet, or computer. Nothing to install, nothing to update.

Will deClusterize access my files or emails?

No. deClusterize never reads, accesses, or connects to your files, photos, or inbox. It's a guided task system — we tell you what to do and you do it on your own device. Your data stays entirely private.

How long does it actually take to get organised?

Most people see a noticeable difference within the first week — typically after 3–4 sessions of 15 minutes each. A full digital reset across all five areas usually takes 3–4 weeks at that pace. The goal is sustainable progress, not a stressful weekend overhaul.

What platforms and devices does it cover?

All of them. deClusterize covers iPhone, Android, Windows, macOS, Gmail, Outlook, Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive, and more. If you use it, we have a guide for it.

What if my digital life is a complete disaster — where do I even start?

Start with our guides — pick the area that feels most overwhelming (photos, files, email, storage, or devices) and follow the step-by-step checklist. Each task takes about 15 minutes. Small steps add up fast.
